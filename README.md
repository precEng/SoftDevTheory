# SoftDevTheory

Repo - https://github.com/odafuta/SoftDevTheory/

Forked from https://github.com/higolab/SoftDevTheory/

The task is to accept Pull Requests based on a unique theme for a top ten list.

# Theme

Top Ten simple, cheap, and reasonably nutritious home meals for a student living alone.

一人暮らしの学生が家で作って食べるご飯。簡単で、安く、そこそこ栄養があるもの。

This is not a general list of Japanese dishes. A classmate already posted "TOP 10 japanese food."

# Listing

1. 納豆キムチ卵かけご飯（味の素と醤油をかけるだけ）
   Natto, kimchi, and egg on rice. Season with Ajinomoto and soy sauce only.
2. サバ味噌煮缶をご飯にのせる
   Canned mackerel in miso, placed on rice.
3. ツナ缶をマヨネーズと醤油で和えてご飯にのせる
   Canned tuna mixed with mayonnaise and soy sauce, on rice.
4. レトルト牛丼を温めてご飯にかける
   Heat a retort gyudon pack and pour it over rice. Gyudon tied for first in macaroni's September 2025 poll of foods to put on rice (301 votes). This entry is that dish in heat-and-pour form.
5. レトルトカレーに冷凍野菜を足して温める
   Retort curry heated with frozen vegetables.
6. 豚こま肉・キムチ・とろけるチーズをご飯にのせてレンジ
   Thin pork, kimchi, and melting cheese on rice, heated in a microwave.
7. キムチとチーズのトースト
   Toast with kimchi and cheese.
8. オートミールを牛乳でレンジし、バナナとプロテインをのせる
   Oatmeal microwaved with milk, topped with banana and protein powder.
9. 無糖ヨーグルトにバナナとプロテインを混ぜる
   Unsweetened yogurt mixed with banana and protein powder.
10. Chicken Teriyaki rice bowl.
11. Open — please add one item by pull request.
    クラスメイトからの1品。useful、clear、かつ 1–9 と重複しないものを1つ。

# How to contribute

Open a pull request that fills item 10 only. Leave items 1–9 unchanged.
